# Setowa visual language

Setowa is the product; LEX is the team. The name joins *Setu* (bridge) and *Wa* (harmony). Product copy should describe the working cleanup-evidence workflow, not imply general media monitoring, sentiment analysis, or automatic impact measurement.

The interface is editorial and restrained: confident sans-serif type, generous spacing, thin dividers, clear hierarchy, and real product UI. Use small monospace labels sparingly. Avoid gradients, glass effects, decorative blobs, and fabricated analytics.

The light palette starts with warm off-white `#F7F2EB`, oatmeal `#EAE2D6`, neutral gray `#EEEEEE`, and pastel sage `#8B9A6E`. Dark mode uses layered charcoal-green surfaces and a softer sage accent, following Catppuccin's idea of distinct base, surface, text, and accent roles rather than copying its colors exactly. Keep body text and controls high contrast.

All synthetic media must remain labeled. A report may contain only approved observations, original evidence links, and explicitly sourced measurements. A weak comparison should show uncertainty rather than a confident claim.
