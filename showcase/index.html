A CLEAR PATH FROM MEDIA TO MEANING

The 5 Pillars of Trust in Setowa.

Every step keeps the source visible and verifiable. AI can propose observations; humans decide what enters the official record.

01 / MEDIA

Media & Frame Analytics

Cloudinary programmable media, responsive delivery, video playback, and sub-second frame derivations without re-encoding.

02 / SKILLS

Contract-Bound Skills

Modular, versioned AI and deterministic skills declaring typed schemas, permissions, and bounded confidence.

03 / WORKFLOWS

Visual DAG Pipelines

Compose multi-node topological workflows with dependency resolution, error boundaries, and telemetry.

04 / REVIEW

Human Verification Gate

Pair evidence, inspect visual split comparisons, and approve findings. AI proposals never become facts without sign-off.

05 / IMPACT

Impact Story & Public Share

Grounded synthesis, chronological timeline spine, and cryptographically secure shareable public presentations.
