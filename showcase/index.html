THE WORKFLOW

From field visit to
defensible report.

The useful part is the chain between a photo and a claim. Every step stays visible to the reviewer.

01 / COLLECT
▣

Organize visits

Attach images to a cleanup site and visit date. Cloudinary stores the originals and delivery links when credentials are configured.

02 / COMPARE
◩

Compare evidence

Select before and after images from the same site. Gemini can draft a cautious visual observation, or explain why comparison is unreliable.

03 / VERIFY
✓

Human review

A named reviewer edits, approves, or rejects each observation. Changing the words or evidence clears the old approval.

04 / SHARE
↗

Export the report

The report contains only approved text, linked images, and measurements someone explicitly recorded.

BUILT FOR TRUST

Good evidence can stand up to a second look.

LEX keeps an AI suggestion separate from the saved human decision. A report is generated from reviewed records, so a rejected sentence cannot quietly reappear.
